## What this PASS establishes

A0 establishes a validated correctness foundation sufficient to remove the roadmap prohibition on moving to the next experimental layer. In particular, `go nodes N` is tested as a fixed node budget, without using wall-clock expiry as a semantic limit.

## What this PASS does not establish

A0 does not establish:

- global game correctness across every possible position;
- search quality or tactical superiority;
- NNUE quality or calibration;
- strength gain against any opponent;
- statistical significance of any strength result;
- correctness of UI rendering or visual presentation.

Those remain separate gates.

## Next gate

The next substantive gate is **Strength calibration**, beginning with A/A neutrality and proper experimental-unit discipline. Search, move ordering and NNUE promotion remain downstream of the strength/correctness methodology and must not be presented as globally stronger merely from directed benchmarks.
